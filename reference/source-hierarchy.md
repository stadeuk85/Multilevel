# Source hierarchy

## Level 1: legislation

Use the Children and Families Act 2014, Part 3, including:

- section 37 on education, health and care plans;
- section 42 on the duty to secure specified special educational provision.

Label findings based on the Act as **Statutory duty**.

## Level 2: regulations

Use the Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014 and current amendments for procedural and content requirements.

Label findings based on the Regulations as **Regulation**.

## Level 3: statutory guidance

Use the SEND Code of Practice: 0 to 25 years as currently published on GOV.UK. It applies to England. Paragraph 9.69 includes the section-by-section content expectations and states that Section F provision must be detailed and specific, should normally be quantified, and must be specified for each Section B need.

Label this basis as **Statutory guidance**, not legislation.

## Level 4: professional evidence

Use reports, assessments and advice supplied for the individual case. Preserve the author, discipline, date and exact recommendation where available.

Label this basis as **Professional evidence**.

## Level 5: local material

Local authority templates, QA frameworks and policies may help interpret local process but must not be presented as national law.

Label this basis as **Local policy**.

## Level 6: editorial practice

Use clarity, coherence, plain-language and document-design principles only after the higher-level sources.

Label this basis as **Editorial good practice**.

## Currency rule

Check publication and update dates. Do not treat a consultation, reform paper, ministerial announcement or proposed Code as law in force.
