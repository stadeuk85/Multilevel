# Section B needs checklist

For each distinct need, ask:

- Is it a functional special educational need rather than only a diagnosis or background fact?
- Is the educational impact described?
- Is it individual to the child or young person?
- Is material professional evidence represented accurately?
- Are separate needs improperly bundled into one broad paragraph?
- Are strengths and context distinguished from needs?
- Is the wording internally consistent with Sections A, C, D and K?
- Is there enough specificity to identify relevant provision?
- Does the need have a stable item ID for golden-thread mapping?

## Common defects

- diagnosis-only entries;
- copied test scores without explaining educational impact;
- behaviour described without underlying need or context;
- omitted sensory, communication, cognition, emotional regulation or physical impact shown in reports;
- contradictory descriptions across sections;
- generic statements that could apply to any child;
- need recorded as provision, such as “needs 1:1 support”.

## Editor boundary

Do not create a new need from general knowledge of a diagnosis. Only identify needs supported by the supplied documents.
