# Legal authorities and official guidance

Use current official versions.

## Children and Families Act 2014

- Part 3 establishes the EHC plan framework.
- Section 37 concerns EHC plans.
- Section 42 requires the local authority to secure the specified special educational provision where it maintains the plan.

Official source: legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6

## Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014

The Regulations supplement the statutory framework for assessments, plans and review procedure.

Official source: legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1530

## SEND Code of Practice: 0 to 25 years

The current GOV.UK publication applies to England. Paragraph 9.69 describes the information to include in each EHC plan section. For Section F it states, among other things, that provision must be detailed and specific, should normally be quantified, and must be specified for each need in Section B.

Official source: gov.uk/government/publications/send-code-of-practice-0-to-25

## Use caution

- Confirm whether legislation or regulations have been amended.
- Do not treat non-statutory checklists as legal authority.
- Do not treat local templates as law.
- Do not treat announced or consulted reforms as enacted.
- Seek specialist legal review for contested classification, enforcement, appeal or remedy questions.
