# Vague-language patterns

This is a diagnostic aid, not a blacklist.

| Pattern | Question for the editor |
|---|---|
| access to | Is access guaranteed, triggered, scheduled or discretionary? Who provides it? |
| regular | How often, for how long and over what period? |
| as required / when needed | Who decides, using what trigger or threshold? |
| opportunities for | How many, in what context and delivered by whom? |
| where appropriate | Who determines appropriateness and by what criteria? |
| support with | What action, intensity, staffing and intended effect? |
| monitor | Who monitors, what is recorded, how often and what follows? |
| encourage | Is there an actual provision, strategy or responsible person? |
| may / should | Is the statement optional, advisory or required? |
| flexible | Which elements may vary and which minimum entitlement remains fixed? |
| available | Is it available in practice at the relevant time and place? |

## Finding test

Only raise a material finding where the complete wording and incorporated evidence fail to answer a question necessary for implementation, accountability or review.
