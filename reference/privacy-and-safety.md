# Privacy and safety

EHCP material may include children's personal data and health information.

## Minimum controls

- remove unnecessary identifiers before model processing;
- use an approved environment and contractual data-processing terms;
- keep model credentials server-side;
- apply least-privilege access controls;
- define retention and deletion rules;
- log access and material human decisions;
- avoid storing raw case files in source control;
- complete an information-governance assessment and, where required, a DPIA;
- provide a human review before any output is used in a statutory process.

## Output safety

- do not diagnose;
- do not infer family characteristics;
- do not imply that a parent, school or professional acted improperly without evidence;
- do not expose personal data in examples, logs or evaluation files;
- do not make an automated decision about statutory entitlement.
