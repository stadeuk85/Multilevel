# Output schema

Return the review in this order.

## 1. Editorial verdict

- Document stage
- Review scope
- Evidence supplied
- Material limitations

Do not give an opaque overall legal score as the primary verdict.

## 2. Three priority findings

For each:

- Finding ID
- Severity: Critical / Major / Moderate / Minor
- Confidence: High / Medium / Low
- Section and location
- Exact draft passage
- Evidence passage, where relevant
- Defect
- Why it matters
- Basis: statutory duty / regulation / statutory guidance / professional evidence / internal consistency / editorial good practice
- Revision task

## 3. Golden-thread breaks

List:

- orphan needs;
- unsupported outcomes;
- unserved outcomes;
- unanchored provision;
- partial threads.

Use stable B, E and F item IDs.

## 4. Evidence-alignment table

| Evidence ID | Recommendation | Draft location | Classification | Missing or altered elements | Human action |

## 5. Evidence requiring clarification

State unresolved conflicts, absent reports and missing facts. Do not fill them in.

## 6. Strengths worth preserving

Quote specific passages and explain the strength.

## 7. Self-check

Confirm:

- no replacement wording produced;
- no facts invented;
- legal authority types distinguished;
- uncertainty stated;
- sensitive information minimised.
