# Output schema

Return the review in this order. The human-readable and JSON versions must describe the same findings, limitations and decisions.

## 1. Editorial verdict

- Review ID and schema version
- Document stage
- Review scope
- Evidence supplied
- Material limitations
- Priority-finding count note

Do not give an opaque overall legal score as the primary verdict. Where fewer than three material findings are supported, say why rather than manufacturing concerns.

## 2. Up to three priority findings

For each:

- Finding ID: P1, P2 or P3
- Severity: Critical / Major / Moderate / Minor
- Confidence: High / Medium / Low
- Section and stable location
- Exact draft passage
- Exact evidence passage, where relevant
- One principal defect
- Why it matters in practice
- Basis: statutory duty / regulation / statutory guidance / professional evidence / local policy / internal consistency / editorial good practice
- Authority citation and checked date, where a legal or local authority is relied upon
- Bounded revision task

A revision task identifies the information or decision needed. It must not provide replacement wording or invented detail.

## 3. Golden-thread breaks

List:

- orphan needs;
- unsupported outcomes;
- unserved outcomes;
- unanchored provision;
- partial threads.

Use stable A, B, E and F item IDs. State confidence and do not upgrade a possible link into a confirmed one.

## 4. Evidence-alignment table

| Evidence ID | Recommendation and location | Draft location | Classification | Missing or altered elements | Human action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classifications are included, partially included, omitted, contradicted or unclear.

## 5. Evidence requiring clarification

State unresolved conflicts, absent reports, missing facts and classification uncertainty. Do not fill them in.

## 6. Strengths worth preserving

Quote specific passages, give their locations and explain the strength.

## 7. Human review

Record:

- decision status;
- reviewer role;
- review date;
- unresolved matters.

Allowed statuses:

- approved for editorial use;
- return for correction;
- specialist review required;
- do not use.

Human approval does not convert the output into legal advice or an entitlement decision.

## 8. Self-check

Confirm:

- no replacement wording produced;
- no facts invented;
- legal authority types distinguished;
- uncertainty stated;
- sensitive information minimised;
- human review completed.
