# Section F provision checklist

For every Section B need, identify the corresponding provision and check the material delivery dimensions.

## Delivery dimensions

- type or nature of support;
- amount or intensity;
- frequency;
- session duration;
- direct or indirect delivery;
- individual or group format;
- group size where material;
- responsible role or service;
- required skill, training or professional expertise;
- programme, equipment, adaptation or environment;
- circumstances or triggers;
- monitoring and review arrangement;
- link to the need and intended outcome.

## Warning phrases

Review, but do not automatically condemn:

- access to;
- opportunities for;
- regular;
- as required;
- when needed;
- where appropriate;
- should;
- may benefit from;
- support with;
- monitor;
- encourage;
- available;
- normally;
- flexible.

The finding must state what material delivery question remains unanswered in the full context.

## Evidence comparison

Where a report recommends specific delivery elements, compare each element separately. A recommendation may be only partially included even when the topic appears in F.

## Editor boundary

Do not choose missing hours, frequency or staffing. Ask the author to retrieve the detail from the evidence or obtain clarification.
