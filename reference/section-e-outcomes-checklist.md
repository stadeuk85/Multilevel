# Section E outcomes checklist

For each outcome, ask:

- What meaningful change is sought for the child or young person?
- Which Section B need and Section A aspiration does it relate to?
- Does it describe an outcome rather than provision, an adult action or a short-term target?
- Is progress observable or assessable?
- Is a baseline or current position available where needed?
- Is the context clear, such as environment, activity or level of support?
- Is the review point appropriate?
- Is the outcome sufficiently distinct from other outcomes?
- For Year 9 and beyond, does the plan consider preparation for adulthood where relevant?

## Common defects

- “will receive” or “will be supported” statements;
- vague aspirations with no observable change;
- percentages or dates invented without evidence;
- outcomes that depend on provision not present in F;
- outcomes unrelated to any identified need;
- provider targets presented as long-term outcomes;
- outcomes that measure compliance rather than participation, communication, independence, learning or wellbeing.

## Editor boundary

Do not write a replacement outcome. Ask the author to identify the intended change, current position, observable indicator, context and review point.
