# EHCP section map

The editor's primary review concentrates on Sections B, E and F, while using adjacent sections for context.

| Section | Editorial focus |
|---|---|
| A | Views, interests and aspirations. Use to test whether outcomes reflect what matters to the child or young person. |
| B | Special educational needs. Extract each distinct functional educational need. |
| C | Health needs related to SEN. Check whether educational consequences belong in B and provision that educates or trains may belong in F. |
| D | Social care needs related to SEN or disability. Use for context and cross-section consistency. |
| E | Outcomes sought. Test meaningful change, linkage, observability and reviewability. |
| F | Special educational provision. Test provision for every B need, specificity, quantification and evidence alignment. |
| G | Health provision. Check classification and links without automatically moving content. |
| H1/H2 | Social care provision. Check classification and links without automatically moving content. |
| I | Placement. Outside the core editorial review unless requested and supported by evidence. |
| J | Personal budget. Check linkage only where relevant. |
| K | Advice and information. Use the listed reports to assess whether evidence is complete and traceable. |

## Classification caution

Do not decide that therapy belongs in F solely from its professional label. Review whether the provision educates or trains the child or young person and whether specialist interpretation is needed.
