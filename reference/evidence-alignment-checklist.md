# Evidence-alignment checklist

## Evidence register

For each report record:

- evidence ID;
- author or discipline;
- date;
- document type;
- relevant assessment findings;
- distinct needs;
- distinct recommendations;
- stated outcomes or intended effects;
- limitations or uncertainty.

## Recommendation audit

For each material recommendation:

1. quote the recommendation;
2. record its evidence ID and location;
3. locate corresponding plan text;
4. compare material elements;
5. classify as included, partially included, omitted, contradicted or unclear;
6. explain the practical consequence;
7. return a human revision or decision task.

## Material elements

Compare separately:

- purpose;
- delivery method;
- frequency;
- duration;
- intensity;
- group size;
- staff role;
- expertise;
- setting or environment;
- equipment;
- review interval;
- responsible body;
- conditions or triggers.

## Conflicts

Where reports disagree, do not merge them into an invented compromise. Report the conflict, identify the plan's treatment and request a recorded decision or further clarification.

## Missing reports

If Section K or the plan refers to evidence not supplied, state that the audit is incomplete. Do not infer the missing recommendation.
