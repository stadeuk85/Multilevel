# Reference factory context

## Job

Hold stable editorial, legal, evidence-review and output-contract material used by one or more workflow stages.

## Rules

- References constrain or inform a stage. They do not own workflow sequence.
- Legal and guidance summaries must point to official sources and state their review date.
- Checklists must not be treated as legislation.
- Examples must remain synthetic and anonymised.
- Real case material must never be stored here.

## Routing

- Legal basis and currency: `source-hierarchy.md`, `authority-register.json`, `legal-authorities.md`
- Safety and privacy: `privacy-and-safety.md`
- Section classification: `ehcp-sections.md`
- Section B: `section-b-needs-checklist.md`
- Section E: `section-e-outcomes-checklist.md`
- Section F: `section-f-provision-checklist.md`
- Evidence comparison: `evidence-alignment-checklist.md`
- Needs-to-outcomes-to-provision mapping: `golden-thread-checklist.md`
- Human and machine output contracts: `output-schema.md`, `output-schema.json`

## Change control

A change to the output contract, legal-source hierarchy or editor boundary requires corresponding updates to examples, evaluation fixtures and release validation.
