# Golden-thread checklist

## Step 1: extract stable items

Create distinct IDs:

- `A-##` aspirations where relevant;
- `B-##` needs;
- `E-##` outcomes;
- `F-##` provision.

Preserve exact quotations and locations.

## Step 2: test links

For each B item:

- Is there one or more relevant E outcome?
- Is there one or more F provision?
- Does the link rely on explicit wording, strong evidence, weak topic similarity or no support?

For each E item:

- Which need or aspiration does it advance?
- Is the intended change supported by the evidence?
- Is relevant provision present?

For each F item:

- Which need does it meet?
- Which outcome does it support?
- Is it provision rather than aspiration, strategy commentary or optional advice?

## Step 3: classify link strength

- **Explicit:** the plan names the link.
- **Strongly supported:** evidence and wording make the link clear.
- **Possible:** topic overlap exists but intent is ambiguous.
- **Not found:** no defensible link.

## Step 4: report breaks

- **Orphan need:** B item without F provision.
- **Unsupported outcome:** E item without a defensible need or aspiration.
- **Unserved outcome:** E item without relevant F provision.
- **Unanchored provision:** F item without a stated B need.
- **Partial thread:** a link exists but material evidence or delivery detail is missing.

## Step 5: no manufactured coherence

Do not add links merely to increase a coherence score. A possible link must remain possible until the author confirms it.
