# Stage 06: priority findings

## Job

Convert the analysis into the most important, actionable editorial findings.

## Inputs

- extracted items;
- golden-thread map;
- evidence-alignment audit;
- recorded limitations.

## Load

- `rules.md`
- `examples.md`
- `reference/output-schema.md`

## Actions

Rank findings by:

1. material need without provision;
2. weakened or omitted professional evidence;
3. provision that cannot be understood, delivered or monitored;
4. outcome incoherence;
5. internal contradiction;
6. clarity and presentation.

Each finding must contain one principal defect, exact draft text, evidence text where relevant, consequence, authority basis, confidence and a bounded revision task.

## Output

Write `06_findings.json` and `.md`. Lead with up to three material priority findings. Where fewer than three material defects are supported, state that explicitly rather than manufacturing findings.

## Completion gate

No stylistic point outranks a material missing need or provision. No finding contains replacement wording or invented detail.
